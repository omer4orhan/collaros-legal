collarOS

collarOS is a small operations workspace for window-cleaning businesses. It helps a team keep its visit schedule, reminders and follow-up work in one place, with a person approving every message before it is sent.

The application is private. It is used by a small number of businesses that have agreed to use it, and it has no public sign-up.

When a business connects a Google Calendar, collarOS reads only the calendar that business selects and writes only minimal team-assignment entries. See the privacy policy for exactly what is read, kept and never done.
